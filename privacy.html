Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

AVR Services Inc. respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be collected when you contact us or use our website and how it may be used.

Information we collect

When you call, email, or submit a service request, we may receive your name, company, phone number, email address, service location, vehicle or unit information, and details about the requested repair.

How we use information

We use this information to respond to inquiries, schedule and perform service, communicate about work, maintain business records, and improve our services.

Phone calls and messages

Calls or messages may be logged, recorded, transcribed, or summarized for service, dispatch, quality, training, documentation, and billing purposes where permitted by applicable law. When required, notice or consent will be provided.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with employees, technicians, service providers, or business systems only as reasonably necessary to provide and document service, operate the website, or comply with law.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable measures to protect information. Records may be retained for operational, billing, legal, and customer-service purposes.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about information you have provided or request a correction, subject to legal and business-record requirements.
